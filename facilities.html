Facilities & Experiences

GOWLI'S INN Homestay offers comfort, recreation, and a serene riverfront experience.

24-Hour Room Service

All air-conditioned rooms with quick assistance whenever needed.

Private Balconies

All rooms have private balcony sitouts overlooking the Cauvery river, perfect for relaxation.

Television With 24/7 Wi-Fi

All rooms are equipped with Color TVs and high-speed Wi-Fi for your entertainment and connectivity.

Bird Watching

Witness a spectacular array of native and migratory birds from around the world. Spot rare species that travel thousands of miles each season, creating a colorful and serene experience for nature lovers.
