Welcome to GOWLI'S INN Homestay

Nestled on the banks of the river Cauvery near Gosai Ghat, just 8 km from Mysore, GOWLI'S INN Homestay offers a peaceful retreat amidst lush greenery. Enjoy spacious cottages with ethnic luxury, modern amenities, and a harmonious connection with nature—perfect for weekends, holidays, workshops, or corporate outings.

Spacious Cottages

Enjoy cozy cottages with ethnic interiors, village-style exteriors, and all modern amenities for a relaxing stay.

Reconnect with Nature

Stroll through lush gardens, enjoy riverfront views, and guided nature walks.

Escape to a riverside homestay where nature embraces you with every breeze and rippl. Wake up to the melody of flowing water and unwind in a world of peace and charm. it's more than a stay - it's a soulful retreat by the water's edge.

In and Around GOWLI'S INN Homestay

Discover historic and cultural sites near Srirangapatna and Mysore.

Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary

Famous bird sanctuary offering a peaceful experience and beautiful sightings of migratory birds.

Daria Daulat

Explore historic fort, palaces, and monuments reflecting the rich heritage of Srirangapatna.

Gumbaz

Historical mausoleum of Tippu Sultan and his family, showcasing impressive architecture.

Sangam

The confluence of rivers, a scenic spot ideal for relaxation and photography.

Mysore Palace

One of India's most iconic palaces, showcasing royal architecture and heritage.

K R S Gardens

Beautifully landscaped gardens with a musical fountain and lush greenery.

Chamundi Hills

Hilltop temple and panoramic views of Mysore city, ideal for sunrise or sunset visits.

Melukote

Ancient town with temples and scenic views, steeped in history and spirituality.

Kunti Hills

Scenic hills perfect for trekking and nature walks near the homestay.

Gosai Ghat

Peaceful riverside spot perfect for relaxation and enjoying river views.

Nimishamba Temple

Historic temple attracting pilgrims and visitors, known for its serene ambiance.

Ranganathaswamy Temple

Famous ancient temple along the river, offering a peaceful spiritual experience.

Shivanasamudra Falls

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Shivanasamudra, a stunning waterfall surrounded by lush greenery and scenic views.
