Rules

Before your stay, please take a moment to read our property rules to ensure a comfortable, safe, and enjoyable experience for all our guests.

Booking Policy

Booking & Property Policies

Booking Policy

  1. Reservations at GOWLI’S INN HOMESTAY can only be confirmed with full payment.
  2. No reservations will be acknowledged without a confirmed call or unless accepted on email by the homestay.
  3. In the case of “No show”, the hotel reserves the right to charge 100% retention against the stay.

Cancellation Policy

  1. No cancellation charges applicable if the booking is cancelled 15 days before arrival.
  2. If cancellation is done between 15 days – date of booking, no refund is applicable and the next room should be booked subject to availability.

Refund Policy

All refunds shall be processed as per the cancellation policy within 7 bank working days.

Check IN and Check OUT

Check IN : 1 P.M
Check OUT : 11 A.M

Early check-in and check-out depend on availability (please contact reservations with details).

Meal Timings

Breakfast: 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM
Lunch and Dinner can be ordered from outside.

Bonfire

Bonfire is available on request and provided at designated areas only.
Available from 7 PM to 10 PM.

Property Rules

  1. All guests above 18 years of age must produce valid ID (Passport, Driving License, Aadhar Card, or Voter’s Identity Card) at check-in.
  2. The management is not liable for any loss or damage to personal items.
  3. Getting into the river is strictly prohibited.
  4. Pets are allowed in the room, Pets must be securely leashed the moment they exit your private room. No exceptions
  5. No smoking inside the rooms.
  6. We do not sell or serve liquor. Consumption of liquor is not allowed on the premises.
  7. Guests are requested to keep the environment clean and dispose of trash/cigarette butts in designated bins.
  8. Guests are liable for any loss or damage to property caused by themselves, their guests, or any person they are responsible for. Payment will be collected at check-out.
  9. The management reserves the right to reject or evict guests causing nuisance or disrespectful behavior.
  10. Children must be accompanied by an adult at all times on the property.
  11. Visiting hotel guests are not allowed in guest rooms without prior notice and approval.
  12. Guests should respect others' comfort, privacy, and peace.
  13. All extra charges (beyond included services) must be cleared before check-out.
  14. The management may apply charges for lost or damaged property/equipment.
  15. Housekeeping is provided daily on request; bed sheets changed every three days or on request with additional payment.
  16. Housekeeping and room cleaning services, beyond daily cleaning, are available on request from 8 AM to 5 PM. Leave the key at the reception.
  17. No Loud Music : No external speakers or outdoor music
  18. Bonfire Hours : 8:30 PM - 10:00 PM (Must be fully out by 10 PM)
